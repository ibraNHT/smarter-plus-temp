# Marketplace Buying Guide (Client)

Use this guide for buying from producers on the marketplace.

## Before you order

- Complete your client profile.
- Confirm your delivery/pickup information.
- Check offer details: title, category, unit, quantity, price, delivery availability, and location.

## Place an order

1. Choose an offer.
2. Enter quantity and any required details.
3. Apply coupon (if eligible).
4. Submit your order.

## Typical order flow

Marketplace orders usually follow this path:

1. Pending validation
2. Confirmed awaiting payment
3. Paid/in preparation
4. In transit
5. Delivered
6. Completed

Some transitions are restricted to client or producer actions.

## Payment

- Follow payment instructions shown during checkout/confirmation.
- Payment status may take time depending on external provider/network timing.

## Delivery or pickup

- If pickup is selected, keep pickup details and timing.
- If home delivery is selected, ensure your address is accurate and reachable.

## Confirm receipt

Only confirm receipt when you have actually received the order.

## Cancel or dispute

- Cancellation availability depends on current order status.
- If there is a serious issue, open a dispute and provide clear evidence.

## After completion

- Leave a fair rating/review where available.
- Check notifications for updates and receipts.
