# Support & Disputes

Use this guide to get help quickly and resolve transaction issues.

## Support channels

- In-platform support sessions
- AI-assisted responses for quick help
- Human agent handover when needed

Support contact: **helpdesk@aheteici.com**

## AI + human handover

- AI may answer common questions first.
- For complex issues, support can be escalated to a human agent.
- AI responses can be imperfect; verify critical actions before proceeding.

## Opening a dispute

If an order has a serious problem:

1. Open dispute for the order.
2. Provide clear reason and timeline.
3. Upload evidence (photos/files/messages) where available.

Only relevant parties and authorized admins should access dispute material.

## Dispute best practices

- Submit factual evidence.
- Avoid duplicate dispute submissions for the same issue.
- Keep communication polite and specific.
- Respond quickly to support/admin follow-up.
