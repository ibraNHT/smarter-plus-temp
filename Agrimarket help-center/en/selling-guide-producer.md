# Selling Guide (Producer)

This guide explains how producers can list offers, manage orders, and get paid.

## 1) Complete producer setup

- Create/complete your producer profile.
- Add accurate business/identity information.
- Wait for validation if your account is pending review.

## 2) Create offers

For each offer, provide:

- clear title and description
- category and unit
- available quantity
- price
- delivery availability
- offer location

Keep listing information current to avoid disputes and cancellation risk.

## 3) Manage incoming orders

You can receive marketplace orders linked to your offers.  
Process them quickly and update status according to platform flow:

- validate/confirm
- prepare
- move to in-transit or pickup-ready behavior as supported

Do not mark status actions inaccurately.

## 4) Negotiation and chat

- You may negotiate where offers are marked negotiable.
- Keep all negotiation in-platform.
- Contact-sharing in chat (phone/email/links) may be blocked by policy controls.

## 5) Wallet and withdrawals

- Track your wallet balance.
- Submit withdrawal requests with your producer payment method.
- OTP verification may be required.
- Withdrawal requests are subject to admin review (approve/reject).

## 6) Best practices for producers

- Keep inventory realistic.
- Respond to order actions quickly.
- Keep proof of delivery/fulfillment.
- Use support channels early if issues arise.

  ## 7) Fees calculation

- All percentages are taken from the line-item subtotal (sum of item prices × quantities), before coupons.

Formulas:

Buyer pays: subtotal + 6% − coupon discount

Producer nets: subtotal − 9%

Platform keeps: the buyer’s 6% plus the producer’s 9%

Example for a 10,000 XAF cart:

Buyer pays: 10,600 (10,000 + 600)

Producer receives: 9100

Platform: 1,500 (600 + 900)

Coupons reduce the buyer’s total only. The service fee is still computed on the pre-discount subtotal; the discount is then subtracted from the subtotal + service fee.

Negotiated chat orders use the same marketplace rates on the agreed price.
