# FAQ (English)

## Account & Access

### 1) Can I register with phone or email?
Registration requires account details including unique contact information; login supports email or phone identifier.

### 2) I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the password reset flow from login, then set a new password using the reset token/link.

### 3) Why can’t I perform producer actions?
Producer actions require a producer profile, and some actions can be blocked if producer status is pending/suspended.

## Orders

### 4) Why is my order not moving to the next status?
Order transitions are role- and state-based. Some statuses can only be updated by specific parties at specific stages.

### 5) Can I cancel any order at any time?
No. Cancellation depends on current status and platform workflow rules.

### 6) Why did my total change at checkout?
Coupon/discount and totals are recalculated server-side; preview values can differ if eligibility changes.

## Coupons & Referrals

### 7) Why was my coupon rejected?
Typical reasons: wrong channel (retail vs marketplace), expiry, minimum amount not met, or per-user cap reached.

### 8) When do referral rewards apply?
Referral rewards are tied to program conditions (commonly first eligible order) and may also depend on active program rules/budget.

## Payments, Wallet, Withdrawals

### 9) My payment is pending. Is that normal?
It can be normal if provider/network processing is delayed. Check status first before retrying.

### 10) Why was my producer withdrawal blocked?
Common reasons: OTP missing/expired, invalid payment method ownership, insufficient balance, below minimum threshold.

## Chat & Safety

### 11) Why was my chat message blocked?
The platform may block phone numbers, emails, and links in chat for safety/anti-fraud reasons.

### 12) Can I negotiate in chat?
Yes, where offer/proposal features allow it. Keep negotiation and agreement details in-platform.

## Support & Disputes

### 13) How do I reach human support?
Start support in-app. AI may respond first, then hand over to a human agent when needed.

### 14) How do I open a dispute?
Open dispute on the affected order and upload clear evidence promptly.

### 15) Who can see my dispute evidence?
Relevant participants and authorized admins only.
