# Orders, Delivery & Pickup

This page explains order progress, delivery choices, and what to do when issues happen.

## Order status basics

Order statuses represent real workflow stages.  
Actions available to you depend on your role and the current status.

Common statuses include:

- pending validation
- confirmed awaiting payment
- paid/in preparation
- in transit
- delivered
- completed
- cancelled
- dispute

## Delivery methods

- **Home delivery:** Ensure address and phone are accurate.
- **Pickup:** Select valid pickup location where required and collect on time.

## When to confirm delivery/receipt

- Confirm only when goods/services are truly received.
- False confirmation can affect your dispute options.

## Cancellation

- Not all statuses allow cancellation.
- If cancellation is available, act quickly before the order advances.

## If something goes wrong

- Gather clear evidence (messages, photos, timeline).
- Use support/dispute flow immediately.
- Keep communication on-platform.
