# Chat, Negotiation & Safety

The in-platform chat supports communication and negotiation while protecting users from unsafe off-platform behavior.

## Chat basics

- Use chat to discuss orders/offers.
- Keep communication relevant and respectful.
- Proposal/negotiation actions may be available on negotiable offers.

## Proposal flow

Depending on feature access, users may:

- send proposal terms
- accept/reject pending proposals
- trigger order creation from accepted proposals

## Safety restrictions

The platform may block messages containing:

- phone numbers
- email addresses
- external links

These controls help reduce fraud and keep transactions protected within platform workflows.

## Best practices

- Keep all payment/order agreements inside the platform.
- Do not share sensitive personal or banking details in chat.
- Report abusive or suspicious behavior via support.
