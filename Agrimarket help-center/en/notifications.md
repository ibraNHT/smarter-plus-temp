# Notifications

Notifications keep you updated about critical account and transaction events.

## What notifications can include

- order updates
- payment/withdrawal events
- support/dispute events
- referral/reward updates
- security alerts

## Delivery channels

Depending on your setup and feature availability:

- in-app notifications
- email notifications
- real-time event pushes

Delivery may be best-effort depending on network/device/provider conditions.

## Managing notifications

- Mark notifications as read in the app.
- Keep email/phone information up to date.
- Check spam/junk if expected emails do not appear.
