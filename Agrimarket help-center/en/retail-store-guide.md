# Retail Store Guide

The Retail Store covers ATI retail offers and uses rules that may differ from marketplace orders.

## What is different from marketplace?

- Retail checkout is specific to retail-designated offers.
- Coupon eligibility is validated by coupon channel (Retail vs Marketplace).
- Fees/order confirmation behavior can differ from marketplace.

## How to buy from Retail Store

1. Open retail offers.
2. Select items and quantity.
3. Choose delivery or pickup (pickup may require pickup point selection).
4. Apply coupon (retail-eligible only).
5. Confirm checkout.

## Coupon rules in retail

- Coupon may work only if channel allows `RETAIL` or `BOTH`.
- Some coupons enforce min-order and per-user usage limits.
- Server-side validation always decides final eligibility.

## Order support

If a retail order has a problem:

- review status and notifications,
- contact support through in-app support channel,
- open dispute flow where applicable for order issues.
