# Getting Started

Welcome to Agrimarket Connect. This guide helps you set up quickly as a **Client** (buyer) or **Producer** (seller).

## 1) Create your account

1. Sign up with your email and phone number.
2. Choose a secure password.
3. If you have a referral code, enter it during registration.

If your email/phone is already in use, registration will fail and you should log in instead.

## 2) Log in and keep your account secure

- Use your email or phone number + password to log in.
- If you forget your password, use “forgot password” and follow reset instructions.
- Keep your credentials private.

## 3) Choose your role path

## Client path (buy products/services)

1. Complete your client profile.
2. Browse marketplace or retail offers.
3. Place orders and track status.
4. Use support/disputes if needed.

## Producer path (sell products/services)

1. Complete producer profile.
2. Wait for producer validation where required.
3. Create and manage offers.
4. Process incoming orders.
5. Use wallet and request withdrawals (OTP may be required).

## 4) Understand marketplace vs retail

- **Marketplace:** You buy from independent producers.
- **Retail Store:** You buy ATI retail offers through dedicated retail checkout.

Coupon eligibility can differ between marketplace and retail channels.

## 5) First actions checklist

- Verify profile details are complete.
- Add delivery/pickup details before ordering.
- Read [Policy Links](./policy-links.md) for Terms and Privacy.
- Save support contact: **helpdesk@aheteici.com**.
